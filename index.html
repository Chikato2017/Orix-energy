Orix Energy specializes in mid-to-large commercial and industrial (C&I) facilities with minimum peak electricity loads of 250 kW up to 100+ MW utility-scale developments. Typical client facilities include manufacturing plants, automated logistics hubs, cold storage warehouses, corporate campuses, data centers, and multi-acre agricultural properties.

Under our Power Purchase Agreement (PPA), Orix Energy fully finances, designs, permits, installs, and maintains the solar and battery storage system on your property with zero upfront capital investment from your business. You simply purchase the clean power generated at a guaranteed, predictable rate substantially lower than your local utility tariff.

Multi-megawatt microgrids and high-voltage substations generally range between 9 and 14 months, with our in-house utility interconnection team accelerating approval cycles.
